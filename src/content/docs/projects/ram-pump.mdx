We built a working PVC prototype for under \$100, and in testing, its filter moved water from pH 5 to pH 9. The poster estimates a full-scale version at \$23,000. The team presented to a panel of industry professionals and received the Subject Matter Expert Award.

Acid forms in old, minimally regulated legacy mines and leaks into waterways. Our proposal collects water from a local source, raises its pH through limestone in the pressure chamber, and pumps it into the mine. A ram pump runs on the flow of the water itself, so it needs no external energy.
