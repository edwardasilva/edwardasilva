I wrote eeprep, a free job-search guide at [eeprep.com](https://eeprep.com/), to help people with their resumes. Its topics include projects, the one-page resume, getting hired for an internship or a first full-time role, and where an AI tool helps in the search. The [short version](https://eeprep.com/advice/resume/quick-setup/) is one page, and each page under it goes deeper on one step.

It links its claims to sources such as the Bureau of Labor Statistics, O\*NET, and NACE, and takes no affiliate fees.

It's an Astro and Starlight site. Scripts check its links, citations, and mobile layout, and Vale checks the prose against its style guide. This portfolio uses the same type, colors, and style guide as eeprep.
