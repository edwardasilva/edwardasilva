I started eeprep to help people with their resumes. It now covers projects, the one-page resume, and getting hired for an internship or a first full-time role.

It links each claim to a source, such as the Bureau of Labor Statistics, O\*NET, or NACE, and takes no affiliate fees.

It's an Astro and Starlight site. Scripts check its links, citations, and mobile layout, and Vale checks the prose against its style guide. This site uses the same type, colors, and style guide.
