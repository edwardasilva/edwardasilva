natlock is the first project I worked on. In June 2021, Cisco ran a two-week externship for high school students and rising college students, and the capstone theme was healthcare. Our group, Group 5, asked how technology could help older patients manage their medication schedules. The deck cites [AgingCare](https://www.agingcare.com/articles/medication-problems-elderly-people-have-146111.htm), which quotes the Department of Health and Human Services: 55 percent of the elderly do not follow their prescription orders.

natlock reminds the patient when to take each dose, and it releases only the scheduled pills. A code chosen by the care team locks the rest away. The deck stops at the design. It has 3D models, a parts list, and a cost model, and it shows no built prototype.

The costs below are the deck's per-dispenser prices after a 10% bulk discount.

| Part | Job in the design | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raspberry Pi Zero | Runs the touchscreen | \$4.50 |
| Mini touchscreen | Shows reminders and takes the PIN | \$13.50 |
| Speakers | Play the sound alerts | \$4.50 |
| Magnetic strip | Locks the door | \$45.00 |
| Meraki temperature sensor | Watches the conditions that preserve the medication | \$27.00 |
| GPS sensor | Tracks the dispenser | \$36.00 |
| SIM card | Not stated in the deck | \$4.50 |
| D batteries | Power, with up to 36 hours on an overnight charge | \$2.25 |
| Biodegradable filament | The 3D-printed body | \$22.50 |

An SOS button sits under a protective cover and notifies the care team. Tia Kachalia's speaker notes say the patient can point it at a chosen person or at 911.

## The cost model

Bella Gigoux presented these numbers. The parts add up to \$159.75 per dispenser. At a price of \$192, each one earns \$32.25, and the closest competitors sell for \$150 to \$600.

The deck's one investment is a \$20,000 industrial 3D printer, so break-even is about 621 units. At 1,000 units, the deck shows \$32,250 in profit, a 161.3% return on the printer. The notes treat labor, warehousing, and logistics as already inside the part prices.

The slide calls \$32.25 a 20% profit margin. That is 20% of the cost and 17% of the price.
